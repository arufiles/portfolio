Research & data analysis

GPR Investigation, Wlingi Dam

Undergraduate thesis · 2026

My thesis used Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR), which sends radar pulses into the ground, to look for hidden irregularities along the slope of the Wlingi Dam.

  • Collected the field data with a 90 MHz GPR antenna.
  • Cleaned the radargrams with several filtering techniques.
  • Read the B-scan profiles and interpreted anomalous responses.
  • Assessed whether they pointed to different subsurface materials or voids linked to possible seepage.
GPR B-scan processing Radargram interpretation

Outcome. The radar showed irregular zones that may be different subsurface materials or small voids. The GPR data alone did not confirm active seepage.
